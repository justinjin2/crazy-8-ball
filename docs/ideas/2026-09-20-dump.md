working name for project: Crazy 8 Ball

* this is a 3D satisfying, chill, competitive, and social 8 ball game on roblox where players can play 1v1s, 2v2s, 3v3s with friends and bots. however there's a twist; players each get an ability that can assist them in winning or sabotaging their opponent. players earn money in each game that they play, which they can use to roll for special and unique cue sticks and custom pool tables. there is a ranking system every player has everytime they play, starting from bronze, silver, gold, platinum, diamond, expert, veteran, master, grandmaster, reyes (rank named after the effron reyes), all go from I to V (excluding reyes), and each level up gives rewards while getting exponentially harder to level up (at the very top, reyes should be one of the hardest ranks people have in which only a few dozen/hundred should have, depending on how many end up playing the game). theres also a trading system for cue sticks and tables as well, and in doing so the economy will have limited time items which will only have certain quantites and be extremely rare, possibly different seasons or seasonal items of different themed sets as more updates come out.

goals and ideas:

* just overarching goals and ideas for the game: this game is meant to reach a broad range of audiences. it caters to the older audience of adults who are familiar with pool and want a chill game to wind down and improve their skills competitively on (roblox incentivizes developers to make games for 18+ by offering 42% more devex), pool lovers looking for a new fresh change of pace of pool games with a unique 3d pool game system and twist to the pool game, and obvious kids (the majority of the roblox platform anyway) in its fun abilities and looking for a new game to play. obviously for all audiences the appeal for this game is the satisfying sound effects, visuals, and gameplay, its replayability, gacha system and economy, socialization aspect, and competitive play. this game is meant to have longevity and a game people can come back to and not just following a trend for a game that would eventually die.
* in a sense this 3d 8ball game is a perfect game meant for roblox. those reasons being that roblox is the only platform where people get to express their avatar in-game, most other 8ball games are 2d or if its 3d its first person and theres no expression for a person. roblox's built in avatar system means theres no need to build a separate avatar catalog if this game were to be made on steam for example. second is market. huge population of roblox players are U.S. and phillipines and europe, which all are huge pool capitals of the world. phillipines itself is considered the pool capital of the world (the greatest player of pool effron reyes is filipino) which is great and encapsulates the market. roblox captures a wide range of audiences for both adults and kids. this game will also be easily great for offline play once roblox adds that feature as well.
* in understanding the roblox audience, this game will focus heavily on a mobile-first design and console and pc compatability. when designing the game always consider all the other platforms with GUIs, mechanics, etc. luckily this game will essentially require a mouse for PC, and the mechanics are intuitive and straightforward for mobile with and console.
* this game will trascend language barriers being simply a pool game which is great, doesnt need heavy english to understand and super intuitive and social.
* This could be the first good 8ball game and respected one everyone goes to. need to have an identity and establish myself as the premier 8ball game of roblox, so that no person could copy it because mine would have too much value

rules and 8ball:

* there should be three levels of difficulty for the 8 ball game, classic, difficult, challenger. classic will just be like the current MVP and gamepigeon way of showing the aiming line, ball trajectory line, and deflection line of where the white ball goes. difficult will have the full aiming line but no ball trajectory line and deflection, and challenger will only have a small aiming line that works if you're close pretty close to the ball, but still no ball trajectory/deflection. players will have the option to select the difficulty level when they create a game, however it should be highly recommended for a player to play only classic until theyre very good. classic is always default selected and the (recommended) option, and difficult and challenger should be locked out until reaching a certain rank.
* rules of the game are just like regular 8 ball, with changes and logistical choices. a coin is flipped to see who will break first. players will have a timer on how much they have to shoot the ball to keep the gaming running quickly (like im thinking at most 20 seconds, needs to be faster pace game but still chill, not too boring that they leave though). there's still scratching of a ball if you foul the white ball into the pocket or dont hit your own color first, and you can place the ball anywhere on the table. hitting 8 ball in ends the game early.
* whichever type of ball becomes yours stripes or solids, the opposite type becomes x'd out like game pigeon to let player know this is not their color to hit. they also get a slight highlight of the balls so you can focus on your balls. there will be a gui that should also keep track of what balls you have pocketed.
* also an button to forfeit the game early however doing so causes you to lose elo. in 2s and 3s need all to forfeit on one team in order to forfeit the game.

gameplay features and mechanics:

* when joining the game, they spawn into the hub and have to walk up to a cue table to start a game. the person who joins an empty table first is considered the host and can select the difficulty, and choice between abilities or no abilities for this game before starting, it should be a simple floating gui popup that they click on. by default abilities are on and classic mode is always selected. when the match starts it will be the hosts' table that is the table if they have a custom table. when the host steps on the pad, they have the choice to either play solo, (playing solo their goal is to just pocket all of the balls for one type first, then after the other type) wait and play with a friend, or an option to play against PC. each has a description that says like all earn rewards. even against a bot they still rank up too. in order to facilitate gameplay it should automatically start in 15 seconds with the bot if no one enters it unless they specifically change the gui option to wait for a friend. or starts automatically when clicking solo. additionally, there willl be an option to easily change servers within the game without having to leave the game. in the future if needed, there will be a global server to match against anyone in the world in a separate server to have a 1v1/2v2, but for now its not prioritized.
* matching up with someone will have a cool versus matchup screen before it starts showing their avatar and cue.
* to simply explain the mechanics for each platform: pc - use hold click while dragging mouse to move angle of ball right and left, use scroll wheel to zoom in and out of camera perspective. use cue gui to drag the cue back to launch, mouse click to add spin similar to gamepigeon/miniclip. mobile will be pinch to zoom in out, swiping left and right, and same as gui as pc to pull finger and drag back to launch with desired power. console will be figured out too, obviously with joystick controlling left and right, up and down to zoom in and out, intuitive ways to add spin and launching the ball.
* abilities: players get one unique ability they roll, it will be a gacha system similar to untitled boxing of different rarities then other. these abilities will be balanced out and wont be detrimental to the game but will make it more fun and add taste. these abilities at its maximum will likely be able to guarantee a ball to go in, or prevent the opponent from being able to pocket a ball. the specific abilities and and number of them will be decided later when planning and getting to it, however whenever making the first ability the rest of them should follow the same framework. make room for this plan for later implementation of these abilities to be decided. just for a few examples, extra bouncy rainbow white ball, magnet towards one pocket, infinite ideas, timestop ability (referencing jojo). these abilities will have timer based cooldowns and can be activated depending on the ability use-case and whos turn.
* streak system, pocket 1, then it starts a streak x1, then streak x2 if second one is pocketed. satisfying sound effect and visual for keeping the streak alive.
* every ball pocketed gives money, bonus money if done a trickshot or for example continued pocketing on a streak more money each time.
* even in solo they earn rewards and money for pocketing each ball, obviously no extra money for winning solo. (this might lead to people making afks to earn money by making macros and what not, which obviously not unintended consquence but not going to be a punishable offense as its also good for playtime)
* i imagine when you step into the queue zone it gives those three options: on the player itself theres a gui thats like "play solo: still earn money" then theres a "wait for a player" and then also below it says "Play against PC: earn rewards and rank still"

ranking system:

* when a player spawns into the game, they start at the hub and can freely move to any table they choose. theres a separate pro lobby meant only for certain rank and above (like diamond 1 as a placeholder for now). it should be noted that past a certain rank it gets exponentially harder to rank, and only difficult/challenger difficulty will be rewarding enough to continue to progress (expert and above should truly be actually skilled players, diamond is a buffer rank).
* generally speaking this elo and ranking system should be thoroughly thought about. in the beginning a player should be able to fly through bronze to platinum, then getting progressively harder to level to reach diamond, and from there they will see it is only useful to continue getting elo from difficult/challenger, as it should be for example like 5-10x the amount of classic. mathematically it should be tried to solve how much elo points do you get per game to determine how long it takes to progress through each rank, how much elo you lose per game and the scale. other things to consider is like for example if you go against a way higher elo person you earn more elo, and vice versa if a higher elo person beats a lower elo they are rewarded a lot less. generally speaking this will create a separation between the casual vs competitive players, while still not like completely segregating.
* we lock out the harder difficultys in order for people to first go through bronze-platinum first which is almost a way to teach them the mechanics of the game before jumping right in, and also preventative measure for kids who dont know.
* in pro lobbies, difficult and challenger should be the only options available. in classic lobbies. there should be failsafes to prevent beginners from accidentally joining a difficult/challenger game. it should give a warning saying like "your rank is not qualified for this difficulty, it is not recommended you play." and a play anyway option.
* even bots give elo and will scale proportionally in skill based on the person's elo
